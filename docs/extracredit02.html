For up to 1.0 points of extra credit, you may do both of the following Extra Credit opportunities by Friday, November 6:

Extra Credit 02-A: Exam Corrections (0.5 Points)¶

Correct 10% (ie. 4 Points) of Exam 02 and then meet with a teaching assistant during their office hours to explain the corrections you made.

If you cannot figure out the correct solutions, then you can alternatively go to their office hours with specific written questions and discuss the solutions.

This meeting should be no more than 10-15 minutes.

Extra Credit 02-B: Web Scraper (0.5 Points)¶

Use functional programming, requests, and concurrent.futures to scrape a website of your choice.

Create a new git repository on GitHub with your scraper and fill out a README.md that:

  • Explains how to use your scraper.

  • Provides a screenshot or video of your scraper in action.

  • Describes the performance impact of using functional programming and concurrent.futures.

Share this git repository in the class Slack channel.
